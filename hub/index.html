How to Upload the Skill

Three minutes, then Claude speaks your brand fluently

  1. Download the moon-water-coastal-design.zip file from the Coastal Design Skill card above.
  2. In your Claude account, open Settings → Capabilities → Skills.
  3. Choose Add skill, upload the zip, and confirm.
  4. Next time you ask Claude for a Moon Water graphic, worksheet, or page, the skill will trigger on its own and your palette and voice will be applied.
